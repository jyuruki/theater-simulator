# v0.23 — Usher comfort and show activity

Small theaters 1, 2 and 9–14 now have one red door at the inner cubby opening,
swinging into the cubby. Row risers sit behind the preceding seat backs, giving
each between-row passage a continuous walking level; side aisles supply the
steps. The A/B drops and ground-level C entry in 3, 6, 7 and 8 are retained.
Blender recliner upholstery extends to the shared armrests, preserving all
1,093 seats. The bar entrance has two swinging service gates.

The clock advances two game minutes per real minute. B shows the pocket break
sheet; T raises a wristwatch for five seconds. The sheet renders after world
signs. The broom and cloth are available at shift start with 1 and 2; the old
task board and supply-cart pickup are removed.

Every seat's tray opens at a new break. Approximately 20% of trays and 12% of
cushions have a spill, and 22% of seats have popcorn. Every tray and cushion
still needs inspection and wiping; an unspilled surface takes about one second
of valid cloth contact. Dirtier surfaces need more work. The order remains
tray, cushion, actual chair debris, close tray, then floor. Existing saves
preserve completed work when moving from v0.22.

Sweeping accepts a shallow downward gaze and reaches about 2.2 meters. Held
tools and cartons retract or slide against nearby walls rather than vanishing.
Work contact still cannot pass through geometry. P or Q previews placement of
bags, liners, cartons and trays: green is supported and clear, red is blocked.
Click, E, or the touch confirm button places the item; P/Q cancels. Rolling cans
still release directly. Put-down state preserves partially used supplies,
opened liners and tied bags.

The supplied Hula trailer plays at each scheduled movie start, at normal speed
with its complete soundtrack. Audio is spatialized from the auditorium, heard
through the entrance when outside, and muffled behind a closed door. Pause and
backgrounding stop the cue together with the shift. Volume and mute share the
existing sound controls. Media loads may need a network connection until the
app's offline cache finishes installing.

Scheduled patrons begin boarding eight game minutes before showtime, allowing
them to walk the hall before the start cue asks the usher to close the doors.
They enter and leave rooms using real paths through the building,
wait at blocked passages, and toss packaging into the movable cans. A bounded
population and reused Blender characters keep this browser release practical.

Long presses on game controls suppress iOS text selection and callouts. The
web manifest, touch icon, standalone display and service worker support a
home-screen app. In Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen, with Open as Web App
enabled where offered. The first complete cache installation needs internet;
later launches can use the cached game and trailer. An update can be applied
from the pause screen, preserving locally saved shift state. This release's
device-specific iPhone behavior still requires checking on actual hardware.
