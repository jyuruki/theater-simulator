# Theater simulator project direction

Read `docs/gameplay-direction.md` before gameplay, interaction, furnishing, or asset work.

The user's goal is to recreate working at Mililani 14, starting as an usher.
Prioritize physical props, embodied tasks, and controls on equipment in the world.
Minimize screen menus. Do not extend the visitor ticket/order prototype into the
main game loop or replace physical work with menu buttons.

Preserve the reference-based building layout unless the requested correction
requires changing it. Check both rendered geometry and player navigation after
architectural edits; data assertions alone have missed visible gaps before.
Use the provided screenshots and clarification history as the reference.
